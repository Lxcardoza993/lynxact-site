Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This website (lynxact.lxlynx.com) is operated by the LynxFlow AI team ("LynxAct", "we", "us"), based in Hangzhou, China. This page explains what data this website does and does not collect.

What we collect

This is a static informational website. We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting scripts, and we do not set cookies for tracking purposes.

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard connection data (IP address, user agent, requested URL) to deliver and secure the site. These logs are retained per Cloudflare's own policies.

What we do with data

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation and any follow-up business relationship.

Open-source code

Our open-source repositories are hosted on GitHub and are subject to GitHub's own privacy policy when you visit or interact with them.

Changes
